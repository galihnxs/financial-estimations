# Pusdatapsy and the UGM network: evidence memo

Internal working notes, 21 Nov 2026. These extend [LPTUI_vs_Unpad_network_evidence_2026-11-21.md](LPTUI_vs_Unpad_network_evidence_2026-11-21.md). Public sources only. **Not for external sharing.**

## Bottom line

1. **Pusdatapsy is a young boutique, not a network or a test publisher.**
   - PT Pusat Data Psikologi Indonesia, Kebayoran Baru, Jakarta. Its domain was registered on 12 Mar 2026.
   - It lists 1 licensed psychologist and 4 assistants.
   - It names 5 clients: PLN, PBESI (the national esports federation), and three private firms in mining, agribusiness and manufacturing.
   - It names no instruments of its own, although it claims in-house psychometric capability and "Indonesian norms".
   - Its weight comes from its President Director, Dr. Rinny Shirley Theresia Wowor, S.Psi., M.Psi., Psikolog. She holds a UI Doctor of Psychology (2016, per a public encyclopedia profile) and a UI clinical master's (2002). The site says she has a record of service in ministries and government agencies and has been an adviser to LBAI and Apsifor (the forensic psychology association). A public encyclopedia profile, which I couldn't verify against an official source, describes her as a senior Polri officer from the Polri Psychology Bureau, seconded to BIN since June 2023.
   - **Read:** a UI-alumni, government and security-sector channel, useful for state and security-sector assessment work. It is not a source of instruments.
2. **UGM is the strongest university on documented, home-grown instruments, and it now runs its own platform.**
   - **UPAP** (Unit Pengembangan Alat Psikodiagnostika) holds a catalogue of 9 tests:
     - PAPS (the graduate admissions aptitude test, accepted for UGM master's entry with a 450 minimum)
     - GMST
     - TKDA
     - visual aptitude test
     - cognitive potential test
     - leadership test
     - individual competency battery
     - general intelligence battery
     - psychology knowledge test
   - Plus **AJT CogTest**, a CHC-based cognitive test for children and adolescents with Indonesian norms. It was built with Yayasan Dharma Bermakna under the guidance of **Dr. Kevin McGrew** (the CHC theorist behind the Woodcock-Johnson tests). UGM certifies AJT testers and launched a group-administered version ("AJT Klasikal") in Sep 2026.
   - **GMPRAISe**: UGM's own digital assessment platform, built with PT Adaptive Solution Tematica and UGM's computer science department. It launched on 25 Sep 2026; GMST is live, AJT digitisation was due in October, and a test centre was planned for November 2026. **This is a platform that competes for the delivery role we want.**
   - **Government reach:** partner of PSKP Kemendikdasmen on the national 2025 Academic Ability Test (TKA). That covers monitoring and evaluation in 6 provinces, an online review across 32 provinces and 320 schools, and psychometric and user-experience studies.
   - **Custom work:** UPAP takes orders for custom test development and runs psychometrics training.
3. **The networks overlap.** AJT Klasikal was co-developed by **Wahyu Widhiarso (UGM)** and **Urip Purwono, M.Sc., Ph.D., Psikolog**, whose publications are Unpad's (TIKI, MPTI). So the Unpad and UGM psychometrics communities share people. Anvesana is also partly based in Yogyakarta.

## What this means for the LPTUI plan

- **UI is still the right lead partner** for brand, regulators and psychologists. But for cognitive tests, **UGM has the deepest documented catalogue**: a CHC battery for children (AJT), scholastic and graduate aptitude tests, and adult batteries. That fills the cognitive gap against Scalify's IKN better than anything visible at UI.
- **UGM will want to own delivery.** With GMPRAISe live, UGM is more likely to offer integration or per-administration access through its platform than to license tests into ours. Approach UGM as a second source for cognitive tests, with an API or integration conversation, not as a white-label supplier.
- **Price leverage.** Credible alternatives (UGM for cognitive tests, Anvesana for the full Scalify set) strengthen our hand when we ask LPTUI for partner rates. Keep this out of anything sent to UI. Use it in the room only if needed.
- **Pusdatapsy is a channel, not a supplier.** It could open state and security-sector assessment work and is UI-alumni-led, which fits a UI-centred story. Its government and security ties are a reputational point to handle carefully. Verify the director's current roles before any approach.
- **Bridge person.** Urip Purwono works across Unpad and UGM. If we need a senior psychometrician who is not tied to Faxtor or Anvesana, he is worth checking for current affiliations and conflicts.
