## Cost basis for BSN (300 participants, 3 instruments)

| Scenario | Cost per participant | Price at 50% net (rounded up to Rp5.000) | Net margin at the current deck price of Rp250.000 |
|---|---|---|---|
| A. As in the deck now: 3 × Rp35.000 | Rp105.000 | Rp250.000 | 50.4% |
| B. A + Excel recap Rp35.000 | Rp140.000 | Rp330.000 | 37.8% |
| C. List price BFI + FTPI + OPTI (Rp110.000) + recap | Rp145.000 | Rp345.000 | 35.8% |
| D. C + tax 11% (TJ is Non-PKP and cannot claim the input tax back) | Rp160.950 | Rp380.000 | 30.3% |

Price formula: cost × 0,9 / (0,882 − 0,50). The 0,9 is 1 minus the 10% partner commission, and the 0,882 is (1 − 2% PPh 23) × 0,9.

Mapping 300 people needs every score in one sheet, so the Excel recap is not optional for us.
