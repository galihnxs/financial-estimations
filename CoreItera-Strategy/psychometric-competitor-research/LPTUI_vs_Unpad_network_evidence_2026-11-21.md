# LPTUI (UI) network vs Unpad-led network: evidence memo

Internal working notes, 21 Nov 2026. Input for a later outward-facing document for Dr. Dicky C. Pelupessy (Dean, FPsi UI). **Do not send this file as is.**

## Bottom line

1. **The hypothesis holds for test publishing.** The Unpad-led network owns about 24 Indonesian instruments with published reliability and norm data: Faxtor has 15, Anvesana has 9. It also runs two commercial channels: Faxtor's licensing and the Scalify platform. UI has no public equivalent today.
2. **The Unpad link is now documented, not inferred.** Crossref metadata lists **Nadim Alfan Assa** with both *Faxtor Indonesia* and *Faculty of Psychology, Universitas Padjadjaran* (FCAT and FMAT papers, SHS Web of Conferences 224, 2025). He is also Head of MeX-Lab at Anvesana and co-developer of IKN and CodeProfile. Whisnu Yudiana (Unpad) co-authors the FMAT paper and is Faxtor's named assessment expert. Faxtor and Anvesana are therefore one Unpad-rooted network. Anvesana's other academic ties (UNISBA, UMM) sit on top of that core.
3. **UI is stronger on institutional weight and is now moving into psychometrics.** UI has:
   - LPTUI: since 1970, BKN accreditation A (2022), 1,000+ corporate clients, custom instruments for Kemenkeu.
   - Since 2021, LPSP3 (UI's measurement-instrument development unit) inside LPTUI via LAIPSI.
   - A "Riset dan Psikometri" unit in LPTUI's org chart, which reports to the Dean.
   - The PsyTRUST measurement lab.
   - In 2026: a "Beyond Likert" Research Day (30 Oct) and PERSEPSI UI 2026 (FPsi UI × LPTUI), which ran a full session "Reimagining Psychometrics: Advancing Human Understanding through Technology and AI" **with the Dean himself as a speaker**.
4. **So the gap is about what is public and productised, not about expertise.** UI has the brand (QS #1 Indonesia in Psychology 2026), access to regulators and BUMN, and data. What it does not show publicly is a digital, normed, licensable test catalogue with technical manuals. That missing layer (platform, report engine, mass delivery, norm-building from real workforce data) is exactly what Coreitera can bring.
