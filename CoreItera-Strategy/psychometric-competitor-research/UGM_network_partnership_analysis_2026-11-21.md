# UGM psychometrics network: partnership analysis

Internal analysis, 21 Nov 2026, built the same way as the Faxtor and Anvesana deep-dives. Public sources only. Not for external sharing.

## Bottom line

- **UGM is the strongest complement to LPTUI, not a replacement.** UGM is very strong where we are weakest: cognitive ability across all ages, an IST-style aptitude test, and situational judgement and leadership tests. It has nothing public for work attitude, personality, learning style or interest. That is exactly the area to ask LPTUI for.
- **The best way in is UGM's validation service, not licensing.** UPAP sells *test construction, test validation (CTT, IRT, DIF, reliability, norms) and test research* as paid services. That fits our core problem: our popular-framework tests have no validation and no Indonesian norms. Commissioning UPAP to validate and norm our own instruments means **we own the validated IP**, and no ongoing licence fee goes to a competitor.
- **Licensing UGM tests into Coreitera is possible but harder.** UGM runs its own platform, GMPRAISe (launched Sep 2026), and sells its tests directly (e.g. PAPS at Rp300,000 per test). Expect UGM to prefer delivering through GMPRAISe or offering an integration, not white-labelling into our platform. It is still worth asking for 2-3 specific tests.
- **Regulatory and government weight:**
  - Ministry of Primary and Secondary Education partner on the national 2025 Academic Ability Test (TKA).
  - The military psychology centre (Puspsi TNI) uses UGM's GMST and is extending cooperation to CAT/CBT apps and data analysis.
  - PAPS is UGM's graduate admissions standard.
  - In the TNI meeting, UGM's tests were valued for being culturally adapted and "not bound by patents".

## Partnership options (ranked)

1. **Commission UPAP to validate and norm our existing instruments.** Start with the digital Kraepelin/Pauli test, then Big Five, RIASEC and DISC. This is UPAP's published service: CTT/IRT item analysis, reliability, DIF fairness checks, norm development and recommendations. We keep ownership, get norms on our own data (2,500 administrations next year gives a real norm sample), and can put "validated by UPAP Fakultas Psikologi UGM" on reports if UGM agrees.
2. **License 2-3 UGM tests for online delivery on Coreitera:** TKDA (adult cognitive), Baterai Tes Kompetensi Individual (SJT) and Tes Kepemimpinan. Ask for per-administration pricing. If UGM insists on GMPRAISe, propose an integration where GMPRAISe delivers and scores, and Coreitera adds the human-in-the-loop report layer.
3. **Use UKP psychologists** for EAP and school talent/interest assessment programmes, as overflow reviewers and counsellors.
4. **Co-develop the missing instruments** (learning style, work attitude) via UPAP's test-construction service, if LPTUI cannot supply them.

## Risks

- **GMPRAISe overlap:** UGM is building its own test centre and may see Coreitera as a competitor for delivery. Lead with validation and reporting, not with "put your tests on our platform".
- **Test security:** PAPS and GMST are admissions tests. UGM will be cautious about exposing their item banks on outside platforms. TKDA, SJT and leadership tests are better first asks.
- **Administration rules:** individual AJT requires certified testers, so it is not suitable for unsupervised online use. The group version is the online candidate.
- **University process:** expect contracts through the faculty and university cooperation offices, and slower timelines than a private publisher.
- **Gaps:** UGM has nothing for personality, work attitude or learning style, so it cannot be our only partner.

## Suggested first message to UPAP (short)

Ask for (1) a quote and timeline for validating and norming our digital Kraepelin/Pauli and Big Five instruments, and (2) per-administration licensing terms for TKDA, Baterai Tes Kompetensi Individual and Tes Kepemimpinan for online delivery, with guidelines, norms and manuals. Mention 2,500 administrations next year from next semester, and offer to fund digitisation where needed.
