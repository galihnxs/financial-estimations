RuleWhat it means for PT CoreItera
0.5% of turnover (PP 55/2022)Only for turnover up to Rp4.8 billion a year, and for a PT only for its first 3 tax years. Since PP 20/2026 (April 2026), a PT can no longer start using it; PTs already using it keep it until their 3 years end. We assume our period is over by 2027 (to confirm).
22% of profit (UU HPP)The normal company tax, on profit after costs, not on turnover.
Pasal 31E cutIf turnover is up to Rp50 billion, the part of profit that matches the first Rp4.8 billion of turnover pays half (11%). So the average rate rises from 11% as turnover grows.
LossesA loss year pays no company tax, and the loss lowers taxable profit for the next 5 years.
PTKPThe personal allowance is for people, not for a PT. It lowers each founder's own salary tax (PPh 21), which is already inside their gross pay.
Cash timingEach month we pay last year's tax / 12 (PPh 25); the rest of last year's tax is paid in April (PPh 29).

Checked against DJP and tax-news sources (DDTC, IKPI), October 2026. Not tax advice: a tax consultant must confirm, especially CoreItera's registration date and whether it ever used the 0.5% scheme.
