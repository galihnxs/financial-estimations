So each psychologist must help more people. Our targets: 33% more clients per bureau (sales agent), 2.5× more assessments per psychologist (Psynthesia, year 2), and 2.2× more accurate diagnosis (from 0.27 to 0.6 agreement, a target).

A2. Bureaus lose clients at night. Clients chat after work; the admin replies the next morning.
