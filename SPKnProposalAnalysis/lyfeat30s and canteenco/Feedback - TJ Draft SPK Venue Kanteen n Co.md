# Feedback: TJ draft "SPK Venue - Teman Journey x Kantin & Co TMG Hotel"

Written 13 Oct 2026. Compared against: Kanteen's WhatsApp offer and our agreed terms (`Final Proposal Summary`).

## Verdict

**Do not sign yet.** The structure is good (clear time table, PIC, tiered cancellation, nett price). But the draft drops what Kanteen already offered, conflicts with our H-5 go/no-go, and leaves privacy and profit sharing open.

## Minor

- "Kantin & Co" is wrong: use **Kanteen n Co**.
- Payment 1 working day after the event is tight: use 7 days.
- Add the forum: PN Jakarta Selatan (as in the Lyfe SPK).
- PIHAK KEDUA should be the legal entity that signs, with proof of authority.
- Kanteen cancels: add "no charge, no cancellation fee".
- Replace the personal phone number with a team number.

## What is good

- Rp50k nett, including service and tax, no separate rent.
- Clear table of date, time and setup.
- Kanteen cancellation refunds the DP in full and helps find a replacement venue.
- A named PIC; photo and video rights; small DP (Rp300k).
