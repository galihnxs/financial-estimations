# Template analysis: "[PROPOSAL TEMAN JOURNEY] Employee Wellbeing - Bank Indonesia.pptx"

Date: 19 Nov 2026. File: repo root, 25 slides, 20" x 11.25" (16:9), 14.9 MB, 62 images.
Goal: decide what to reuse for the new **Product Knowledge deck** (Teman Journey + Coreitera).

## 1. The answer

The template is a good base. About **17 of 25 slides can be reused**: 5 as they are (company profile, philosophy,
principles, legality, closing), 9 with small edits, and 3 as layouts for new content. The EAP part (slides 11-13,
17-18, 22-24) is already a complete product story and becomes the model for every other product family.
What it lacks: training, assessment and Coreitera prices, a product map, and the selling rules.

Slide 23 promises MHCU screening for 70 employees each quarter in Jeda Tahunan. This is cheap: the test costs at most
Rp7k (Galih, 19 Nov 2026), so 70 employees add about Rp490k a quarter. The master sheet now models 70. Jeda Tahunan's
low margin (17%) comes from the 15 counseling sessions, not the screening.

## 2. How it is built

| Item | What we found | What it means for the new deck |
|---|---|---|
| Origin | Canva export. Every slide uses the "Blank" layout; content is free shapes, groups and pictures | No reusable PowerPoint layouts. To reuse a look, we duplicate a whole slide and change its text |
| Background | Navy-to-steel-blue gradient with soft circles (one image per slide) | Keep: it is the TJ look |
| Fixed elements | TJ logo top left, date pill "Oktober 2026" top right | Keep on every slide; change the date |
| Colours | Navy `1C4587` / `0A1552`, white text, sand cards `E8D5B0`-like, white rounded cards, maroon band on cover `9A3A20` | Use as the deck palette. Coreitera slides can add its teal `0F7A73` as an accent |
| Fonts | Aileron (352 runs), Canva Sans / Canva Sans Bold, Glacial Indifference, Poppins | Canva fonts are often missing on sales laptops: PowerPoint will swap them. Either embed them, or present from Canva / PDF |
| Text size | Body mostly 21 pt on a 20" slide (= about 16 pt on a normal slide); titles 45-91 pt | Fine for reading on screen; dense for a live talk |
| Components | Big white title, sand note cards, white rounded package cards, highlighted (sand) recommended card, comparison table, circle photo + credential card, client logo wall | All reusable for other product families |
| Images | TJ logo, BI logo, 2 training posters, 8 psychologist photos, MHCU sample report, client logos, UI logos | Reuse everything except the BI logo on the cover |

## 5. Pricing problems found in the template

| # | Slide | Problem | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 23 | Jeda Tahunan includes MHCU for 70 employees per quarter | Corrected: at Rp7k per test this is about Rp490k a quarter. Cost per quarter Rp8,7 jt; net margin 17%, below 30% because of the counseling sessions (15 x Rp385k). Needs Director approval when sold |
| 2 | 23 | "Rp11.00.000" | Typo, should be Rp11.000.000 |
| 3 | 23 | "Nilai normal Rp15.000.000 per term" while Jeda (3 months) is Rp12 jt | The "normal value" anchor is not on any price list |
| 4 | 22 | "MHCU individual Rp70.000 / pegawai" | Still correct: stays Rp70.000 (cost about Rp34,5k, margin 44%) |
| 5 | 17, 18 | Tumbuh and Seperjalanan say "1x / 2x skrining MHCU" without a headcount | Low cost risk now (Rp7k per test), but state a headcount so the scope is clear (model: 8 and 12) |

## 6. Recommended build

1. Copy the template, keep slides 2-5, 12-17, 19-21, 24-25, and duplicate the layouts above for the new slides.
2. Remove client-specific slides (9, 10) and the BI logo.
3. Fill every price from `Master_Produk_dan_Harga.xlsx` (Harga Daftar Baru). Show list prices only: no cost, margin or commission in the deck.
4. Selling rules go on an internal appendix slide, which can be hidden when the deck is sent to a client.
5. Target length: about 30-32 slides in 7 sections: Tentang kami, Peta produk, Pelatihan, EAP, Asesmen, Psikoedukasi dan jasa ahli, B2C, Coreitera, Bukti (psikolog, klien), Aturan jual (internal).
