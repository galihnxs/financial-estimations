## 1. Answer

1. **Your instinct is right: lock Kanteen n Co in writing before signing Lyfe.** The Lyfe SPK makes Teman Journey (TJ) provide the venue, MC and all production "at no cost" to Mega. So every venue surprise lands on TJ and nobody else.
2. **The ticket price is not the main problem.** At Rp245k and 12 people, net profit is about Rp927k (target Rp1M). The venue can move this number by Rp1-3M. Price moves it by Rp100k.
3. **Do not raise the ticket to Rp299k.** The RAB's Rp325k / Rp299k were all-in prices *with* the book. The SPK splits the book out (Rp69k, paid to Mega). Rp245k + Rp69k = Rp314k for the buyer, which is already near the old Rp325k bundle.
4. **Time is the real constraint.** Both posts must go live by **17 Oct (4 days)**. Kanteen must be written and signed by **16 Oct**.
